HACKATHON PROJECT · HC-02

HeatCare AI

Predicting Hospital Demand During Extreme Heat

Supporting smarter hospital preparedness during extreme heat.

Problem

Extreme heat can increase heat-related illnesses and emergency visits, placing pressure on hospital beds, medical staff and essential resources. Hospitals need ways to anticipate potential demand and prepare ahead of time.

Our Solution

HeatCare AI is a healthcare preparedness dashboard that brings together available environmental information, hospital data and experimental demand forecasts. It helps users review expected demand, monitor capacity and identify potential pressure points.

Key Features

  • Dashboard overview of heat and healthcare indicators
  • Experimental short-term hospital demand forecasting
  • Hospital bed and resource capacity monitoring
  • Risk indicators and demand-pressure alerts
  • Weather context and interactive satellite thermal-anomaly map
  • Charts and visual analytics

Technology Stack

React · Vite · CSS · Python · FastAPI · Pandas · NumPy · Scikit-learn · Recharts · React Leaflet

Disclaimer: HeatCare AI is an educational prototype. Demonstration data and experimental forecasts are not validated for real-world clinical or hospital operational decisions. NASA FIRMS satellite thermal anomalies are not direct measurements of ambient temperature.
